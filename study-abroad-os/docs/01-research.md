# Research & Validation Plan

This document captures the current validation approach. Research is not considered complete.

## Discovery research priorities
Study real conversations across Reddit, forums, social communities, and credible reports to identify:
- where students start research
- tools/sites/groups they use
- scholarship discovery pain
- eligibility uncertainty
- stale/conflicting information
- deadline/document workarounds
- agency value, pricing, trust, and conflicts of interest
- need for pre-departure peer connections
- housing scams and other arrival risks

## Research stages
Discovery -> Shortlisting -> Scholarship/Funding -> Application -> Offer/Decision -> Visa/Pre-departure -> Arrival/Community

## Validation philosophy
Community research is discovery. Thai interviews/surveys are for confirmation of Thailand-specific assumptions and gaps that global research cannot answer.

## Key hypotheses still requiring evidence
- Thai students experience significant information fragmentation.
- Personalized eligibility and next-action guidance is more valuable than a large raw database.
- Community has different value by stage and may become strongest after an offer.
- Students will pay for on-demand human help rather than only full agency packages.
- Trust and source freshness materially influence product choice.
- Safer, verified housing is a meaningful post-offer expansion opportunity.
