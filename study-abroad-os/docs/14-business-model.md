# Business Model Hypotheses

## Free core
- university/program discovery
- scholarship discovery
- basic eligibility
- basic journey
- community

## Paid software / AI
Potential subscription or seasonal package for advanced matching, AI advisor, document organization, deadline automation, and advanced planning.

## Human help
On-demand student mentor sessions. Platform may take a transparent commission.

## Later services
- expert/professional services
- translation
- interview preparation
- housing referral/booking revenue where appropriate

## Principle
Revenue must not silently corrupt ranking or trust. Sponsored results must be labeled.
