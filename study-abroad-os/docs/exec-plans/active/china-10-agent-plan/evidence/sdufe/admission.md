## Route boundary

This is the 2026 English-taught Bachelor’s **International Economics and Trade** route in SDUFE’s international undergraduate/special scholarship materials. The special scholarship guide lists International Economics and Trade alongside Cross-border E-commerce and Business Administration; each listed program needs at least 20 students to commence. It says a student may apply to transfer majors if a program does not open. This report covers only International Economics and Trade. It does not generalize its IELTS or bundled fee to SDUFE’s Chinese-taught and other programs.

The general 2026 undergraduate brochure lists English bachelor tuition CNY 21,000/year and double-room accommodation CNY 750/month/person. It is unclear whether the general fee table is inapplicable to, superseded by, or additional context for the special scholarship program. Preserve the direct route-specific bundle and request reviewer/source-owner resolution; do not split it.

- No exact age, GPA, program duration, opening date, deadline hour/timezone, numeric scholarship threshold, quota, total funding period or 2027 criteria were found in checked official sources.
