## Award in this catalog record

The record lists one award: **English-Taught Undergraduate Programs Special Scholarship** (English official title) / **国际学生英语授课本科专业专项奖学金** (Chinese title). SDUFE’s 2026 guide explicitly names International Economics and Trade among the English-taught bachelor programs under the award. The route is therefore confirmed for that degree/program/language at category level. The guide says each program needs at least 20 students to commence; it does not publish the actual cohort count or scholarship quota. Non-Chinese citizens are eligible as a broad group. Thai citizens are not named separately; Thai applicability follows from the stated non-Chinese-citizen rule if the applicant is classified by SDUFE as non-Chinese and meets the other conditions. This is an inference, not a Thailand-specific award promise.

### Admission rules versus scholarship rules

For 2026 admission, SDUFE’s general international undergraduate guide [E3] separately requires non-Chinese citizenship, recognized high-school graduation/equivalent, and for English-taught programs IELTS at least 5.5 or equivalent; native English speakers are exempt. A program with fewer than 20 students will not open, with transfer as an option. The special scholarship guide repeats non-Chinese status, high-school diploma and IELTS 5.5/equivalent, then adds no concurrent scholarship and merit-based selection/continuation rules. CSCA and HSK are not admission requirements in the checked general guide; the scholarship guide lists them only as optional evidence for higher-level award consideration. Meeting admission requirements does not guarantee scholarship selection.
