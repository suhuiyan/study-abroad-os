## Separate related award: 2026 CSC Silk Road Program

This is not the BISS listed in the catalog and must be represented separately if considered. BLCU's 2026 Silk Road notice explicitly lists Thailand among eligible countries and lists “Chinese Language (Chinese Language Direction, Economics and Trade Direction)” among eligible bachelor majors. Eligibility includes non-Chinese nationality, high-school completion/equivalent, age generally no more than 25, and no simultaneous receipt of another Chinese Government Scholarship. Listed coverage: tuition exemption, free on-campus housing, comprehensive medical insurance and CNY 2,500/month undergraduate living stipend. Its application deadline was 2026-04-20 17:00 Beijing time; this 2026 call is closed and is not evidence for March 2027. (S6 §§I–IV.)

For S6, funding duration/renewal is not detailed on the cited notice beyond the scholarship category; result is competitive and subject to BLCU selection and CSC final review. Do not combine S6 benefits with BISS or claim both can be held. The Chinese+ teacher scholarship (S7) is not applicable to this catalog route: the supported bachelor majors are Translation and Translation Localization, not Chinese Language.
