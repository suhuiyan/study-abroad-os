## Applicability, distinction and reconciliation

- The 2026 brochure is GC-specific and identifies an international undergraduate admission route, so the three tiers are confirmed as published for that route in 2026. Its materials do not explicitly name Thai citizens or state nationality-by-nationality eligibility. For a Thai applicant, direct eligibility therefore remains `applicability_unclear` pending SJTU confirmation; do not translate “international” into a confirmed Thai-specific award rule.
- The GC FAQ says international students may apply through GC and says no separate application is needed for the “SJTU International New Students Scholarship”. Neither the FAQ nor the class table explicitly states that this label is identical to each First/Second/Third-Class award. Record the application mechanism as needing review at tier level.
- General conditions to be officially admitted, not concurrently receiving another scholarship, and ranked on academic/interview performance are in the 2026 GC fees page. The brochure provides extra criteria only in the First-Class row; blank Second/Third criteria cells are not evidence that they have no criteria.
- The 2026 SJTU ISC annual review notice (S4) concerns current undergraduate holders and does not establish new-student award selection, GC eligibility, or renewal terms. GC’s broader scholarship-financial-aid page could not be fetched (S5); mark this research limitation, not a negative finding.
- Catalog has no deadline and describes the three tiers in prose. Suggested catalog handling: retain those terms only as 2026 and pending review; store monthly amounts and four-year duration separately by tier; do not create a scholarship deadline or 2027 entitlement. Keep Thai applicability, tier-to-FAQ route mapping, renewal and 2027 facts unknown.
