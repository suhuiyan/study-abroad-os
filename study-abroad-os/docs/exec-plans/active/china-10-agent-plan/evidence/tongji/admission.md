## Catalog reconciliation proposal (no product edits)

- Keep the single catalog ID, but name the exact International School route and its two 2026 directions; do not apply this HSK rule to other Tongji majors.
- HSK 4 >=180 is directly specified for first-year entry in this route and overrides the guide's general HSK 5 >=180 rule for this route. The catalog’s two-year validity needs separate confirmation for HSK 4.
- Keep CSCA required for 2026 undergraduate admission, but do not assert specific subjects until the linked major spreadsheet can be reviewed.
- Preserve the Jan 10 vs Jan 20 opening-date conflict; the Mar 31, 2026 closing date is corroborated. Do not project either into 2027.
- Leave duration, exact route tuition, registration fee, and 2027 terms unknown. Treat Thai nationality under the published non-Chinese-citizen rule conditionally, subject to the listed nationality-history requirements and university pre-review.
