## Conflicts, unknowns and catalog reconciliation

- Shanghai Government Scholarship has an explicit exclusion for language/literature programs of the applicant’s native language or a “third language”. Tongji does not explain how its Chinese Language undergraduate major is classified for this exclusion. Do not mark this scholarship as definitely applicable to the catalog route until Tongji confirms.
- S1 and the 2026 undergraduate guide establish scholarship admission scope, while the application guide independently sets admission requirements. Do not treat HSK 4, CSCA, or other admission conditions as scholarship-specific selection criteria without a direct scholarship rule.
- For Shanghai scholarship, preserve category-specific coverage. Do not merge A and B coverage or calculate a total grant. Amounts are 2026 terms only; scholarship support period is not stated.
- For CSC Type A, the route’s Chinese-taught undergraduate status fits Tongji’s broad program category, but award terms and Thai eligibility depend on Thailand’s official call. No country-specific deadline or amount has been verified.
- Suggested catalog handling: retain both awards only as 2026 source facts with status requiring review for the exact Thai/Chinese Language route; keep deadline empty for 2027. Separate SGS A/B benefits and keep CSC amount, duration and Thai cycle eligibility unknown. No production/catalog edits made.
