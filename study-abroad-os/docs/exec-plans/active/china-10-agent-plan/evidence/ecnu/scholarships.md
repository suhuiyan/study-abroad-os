## Conflicts, unknowns and catalog change proposal

- **English/Chinese age discrepancy:** EFS English source says under 25; Chinese counterpart says no more than 25. Preserve as `conflict` pending reviewer interpretation; do not flatten to either “<25” or “<=25”.
- **Award label in admission summary:** S5 search-rendered table text combines “Shanghai Government Scholarship ECNU Excellent Freshmen Scholarship.” S2 lists two separate awards, S4 gives separate rows/benefits, and S6 has its own SGS page. Treat this as a presentation/label ambiguity and keep awards distinct. Ask ECNU to clarify whether the EFS award is independently administered or funded under an SGS arrangement if that distinction affects student eligibility; do not combine their monetary coverage.
- **2027:** no 2027 EFS notice or deadline located as of retrieval. All 2027 applicant rules, cycle, HSK, amount, duration, quota, forms and deadline are unknown (`not_found_in_checked_sources`). Recheck official ECNU pages.
- **Applicability:** General target is full-time undergraduate; named exclusions are joint and English-taught programs. Chinese-taught status is not excluded, but exact program/major and Thai individual's nationality status require case-level confirmation (`applicability_unclear`).
- **Catalog proposed changes only:** retain EFS award name and 2026 stipend 2,500 CNY/month for 12 months; explicitly state first-year stipend only and no tuition waiver stated in this award's coverage; keep award selective and avoid guarantee; keep 2027 deadline empty/unknown; distinguish award HSK 5 from admission HSK; flag age conflict; do not fold SGS Type A/B tuition, housing or insurance into EFS. Catalog is not edited.
