### Admission dates are not scholarship dates

For context only, S2 §II gives the 2026 **admission** window as 2025-12-01 through 2026-02-28, with certain major deadline exceptions of 2026-05-31; all dates Beijing time and inclusive. S1 §I is the scholarship-specific source and says scholarship applications follow their own schedules. Its general ZGS date is malformed and therefore remains conflict/unknown despite resembling S2’s admission window. Do not project an admission deadline onto the scholarship. For Chinese Language (International Students), S1 explicitly aligns the ZGS deadline to 2026-05-31. No exact cutoff hour is stated.

## Contradictions, unknowns, and catalog review

1. **General 2026 ZGS deadline conflict:** S1 §I says `2025-12-01` through `2025-02-28`, impossible chronology. S2’s general admission deadline is `2026-02-28`, but that is an admission deadline, not proof of the scholarship deadline. Leave the general scholarship date null/conflict (`contradictory`) until ICZU clarifies.
2. **Chinese Language exception:** S1 §I explicitly ties the Chinese Language (International Students) award deadline to `2026-05-31`. This is a major-specific 2026 scholarship date and should not be generalized to every Chinese-taught major.
3. **2027:** no 2027 award rules/deadline/application dates found. Keep each 2027 field unknown `not_announced` even though some site indexes remain live and admission pages exist.
4. **Coverage detail:** RMB 20,000 for one year is stated, but installment timing, exact cost category, quota and awardee count are not. Do not describe it as full tuition, annual stipend, or guaranteed funding.
5. **Thai applicants:** rules are written for international applicants generally, not specifically Thai nationals. Applicability is confirmed conditionally through the published non-Chinese-citizen/foreign-passport admission rule and any relevant nationality-history restrictions; no extra Thai-specific test or award rule was found.
