## Conflicts, unknowns, and catalog reconciliation

1. **Cycle mismatch in old catalog record:** `zju-chinese-undergraduate` has no explicit cycle and contains a claim “CSCA from 2026/27”; the source is a 2026 guide. Keep that claim as a 2026/27 applicability statement only. All 2027 application dates, offerings, major-level requirements, tuition and scholarship terms remain unknown.
2. **Multiple majors:** route has differing HSK, CSCA subject combinations, duration and tuition. No single HSK/GPA/tuition/duration value can accurately represent all majors. The record’s current generic treatment must keep “select major” qualification visible.
3. **ZGS deadline conflict:** S3’s displayed general endpoint is 2025-02-28 (likely a source typo, but this research does not correct it). The Chinese Language major’s stated 2026-05-31 is internally plausible and agrees with S2, yet the broad award deadline needs official clarification. Do not publish a single scholarship deadline as verified.
4. **City:** Hangzhou is the admissions office address; at least Ocean Engineering and Technology moves to Zhoushan for years 3–4. Route-wide city is not safely a single value.
5. **No universal GPA or CSCA score threshold, accommodation charge, score validity period, or exact cutoff hour** found in checked sources. Mark unknown / `not_found_in_checked_sources` rather than infer.
