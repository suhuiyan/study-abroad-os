## 2026–2027 in-study scholarship: distinct track, not the entrant route

S3 (UIBE notice dated 2026-05-07) explicitly restricts the award to self-funded international undergraduates/postgraduates/doctoral students in **Chinese-taught** programs who have already studied for at least one year. Age is calculated as of 2026-05-06; undergraduate maximum 25; no concurrent government/organization/university scholarship except one-time awards. Categories A/B/C provide the coverage bundles listed in the notice (A: tuition, accommodation, living allowance, insurance; B: tuition, accommodation, insurance; C: tuition and insurance). The notice gives no numerical CNY amount for the living allowance. For this record, mark `not_applicable` because the program is English-taught and the target user is a new entrant, regardless of the academic-year title. Its 2026-05-11 deadline applies to this current-student track only and must not be used as a new-student deadline.

## Conflicts, unknowns, and catalog proposal

1. **English-route HSK applicability:** S2's 2026 new-student scholarship notice says degree scholarship applicants require HSK 4. S1 is English-taught and does not list HSK as admission material. This is an award-vs-admission distinction, not a contradiction: HSK 4 may be scholarship-only. The notice does not explicitly exclude English programs, but it routes submissions to a Chinese-program email. Leave award eligibility for International Business `needs_review` pending UIBE confirmation.
2. **New-student vs in-study award:** S2 (2026 new students) and S3 (2026–2027 in-study, Chinese-taught, ≥1 year enrolled) are different competitions. Do not transfer deadlines, criteria, or coverage between them.
3. **Amount and renewal:** S2 lists benefit categories but no stipend amount, fixed award value, quota, duration or renewal rule. S6's old one-year/half-year tuition-waiver terms are 2022-only and must not fill these unknowns.
4. **Existing catalog:** current `uibe-international-business` record contains no scholarship entry. Do not add an award as confirmed. Proposed future value: add Beijing Government Scholarship as 2026 `needs_review` only after coordinator/reviewer accepts applicability; otherwise leave record without a confirmed scholarship and retain evidence of not-found/uncertainty. Keep 2027 unknown. Historical UIBE Freshman Scholarship is not a current-cycle fact.
