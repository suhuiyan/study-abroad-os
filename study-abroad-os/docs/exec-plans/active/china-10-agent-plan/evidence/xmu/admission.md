## Route boundary

The existing record groups Chinese-medium undergraduate humanities and business/economics-related majors; it is not one university-defined program. The 2026 Chinese-medium major directory lists individual programs, campuses and durations. The admissions guide assigns different HSK and tuition rules to categories, and explicitly sends the Chinese Language major to the Overseas Education College for its separate HSK requirements. Use this record only as a route/category index; a selected major is needed before treating language, tuition, exact duration or campus as applicant-specific. The official guide says lower-year large-category cultivation precedes entry to the admitted major. The current directory page is labeled 2026; it does not establish the 2027 catalog.

## Catalog comparison / proposed updates

Existing catalog values are leads, not evidence. Proposed actions for a later reviewed catalog revision:

- Keep the route identity broad, but label it as a grouped route requiring major selection; do not imply that “humanities and business” is one named official major.
- Keep general Chinese-medium threshold HSK 5 / 210 only for the 2026 humanities, economics, management, law and arts categories. Add the documented high-school Chinese-medium certificate alternative. Mark Chinese Language/OEC as a separate threshold that needs its own source; do not propagate HSK 5 to it.
- Split tuition by category: humanities RMB 24,000/year; economics/management/law and OEC Chinese Language RMB 26,000/year. Exact major still required. Catalog’s single fee obscures this split.
- Correct CSCA description to compulsory Professional Chinese and Mathematics for Chinese-medium programs, with stated Chinese Language/HSK4 exception; the guide states no minimum score. “Additional subjects by major” is unsupported here and should not be presented as a verified rule.
- Preserve 2026 dates distinctly: opens 2025-12-01; self-funded deadline 2026-05-10; XMU New International Students Scholarship deadline 2026-04-10; CSC University Program deadline 2026-02-15. Scholarship dates must not be used as admission deadline.
- Revise scholarship facts to age <=25 at 2026-09-01, competitive undergraduate tuition waiver, five total undergraduate places, 4/5-year duration by program and annual review. Treat the catalog statement as cycle-specific and do not guarantee award.
- Leave all 2027 values unknown pending a 2027 official guide/notice. Catalog age 18–30, application dates, language, CSCA and fees cannot be rolled forward.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Catalog vs. cycle evidence:** catalog’s `opensAt=2025-12-01`, `deadline=2026-05-10`, HSK 5/210, age 18–30, application fee RMB 400 and named scholarship align with 2026 evidence only. Its combined admission/scholarship deadline note is ambiguous; keep separate fields.
- **Tuition scope:** catalog’s single RMB 26,000/year is wrong for humanities category (RMB 24,000/year) and valid only for specified categories, including economics/management/law and OEC Chinese Language. Major selection is required.
- **CSCA scope:** source establishes Professional Chinese + Mathematics for Chinese-medium programs and no score minimum, not a general “extra subjects by major” rule for this route.
- **2027:** all fields not supported by an official 2027 cycle source remain unknown; 2026 evidence is not reusable as 2027 verification.
- **Major-specific:** exact degree title, campus, duration, tuition category, and any program-specific academic prerequisites cannot be resolved while the record groups multiple programs. No numerical GPA threshold, HSK score validity, or exact application cutoff time was found in the checked sources.
- **Scholarship:** official five-place quota is undergraduate-wide, not a route-specific promise. No undergraduate stipend/housing coverage or extra scholarship-specific CSCA/language threshold is established by the 2026 XMU notice.
