## Other PKU scholarships and CSCA boundary

The 2026 route notice also describes the Chinese Government Scholarship (CSC Type A), a distinct scholarship that is not one of the catalog's PKU/Beijing pair. S1 §VII.1 requires valid CSCA results for CSC applicants; Type A application is through the home-country dispatching department and the CSC system, with PKU as first choice; recommended candidates undergo PKU review/interview. Coverage is tuition, accommodation, insurance and monthly living expenses. Amount, stipend schedule, quota, Thai dispatching-body process and 2027 dates are not established by S1. Record it separately if the catalog scope is expanded; do not merge its CSCA condition or application route into the PKU or Beijing awards. The ordinary entrance examination itself is separate from this scholarship rule.

## Catalog delta and unresolved items

Catalog currently has one combined `PKU / Beijing Government Scholarship` item, describes full/partial tuition, insurance and living costs “by type,” and says university assessment/no separate application. Proposed correction: split into two award records; state that new admitted undergraduates are assessed without a separate award application in the 2026 entrance-exam route; distinguish PKU stipend/insurance/tuition (housing not evidenced) from Beijing Types A/B/C; mark award as selective, never guaranteed. Keep 2027 deadline blank/unknown. Add the Beijing no-other-award/regular-tuition terms and PKU annual review/no-other-funding terms only with their scope qualifications. Do not enter a stipend amount, quota or duration guarantee. Flag PKU's general four-year scholarship page against the 4-6-year undergraduate route duration for review.

No applicable award was absent from this search: the 2026 route notice directly names both awards. However, Thai-specific eligibility details, 2027 terms, precise stipend amounts, quota, exact prospective-undergraduate deadline, and PKU scholarship duration for a selected 4-6-year major remain `unknown` / `needs_review` as identified above. This does not claim these awards or other PKU scholarships are unavailable.
