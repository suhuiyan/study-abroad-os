## Conflicts, unknowns, and catalog delta

- **2027:** No 2027 entrance-exam notice appeared in the official archive S3 as of 2026-09-23. All 2027 dates, eligibility reference dates, fee, tuition, documents and scholarship terms remain unknown with reason `not_found_in_checked_sources`. Recheck official PKU notices before application; do not carry forward 2026 values.
- **Route distinction:** S4 separately lists admission without written examination. Its standardized-test/HSK/CSCA rules do not belong to this catalog route. The standard entrance-exam notice requires a written preliminary examination and later secondary examination.
- **CSCA distinction:** S1 §VII attaches CSCA to Chinese Government Scholarship applicants; it does not make CSCA a general entrance-exam condition. Catalog `csca: "scholarship"` is directionally supported, but specify the CSC-only scope and subjects.
- **Catalog proposed changes:** retain official identity and Chinese-taught route; replace generic age note with explicit 2026 age reference and special nationality-history caveat; explicitly scope dates, fee and exam stages to 2026; keep tuition null/unknown; keep 2027 dates empty/unknown. Keep HSK/IELTS unknown rather than inventing thresholds. Replace generic scholarship coverage with separate CSC, PKU and Beijing scholarship records or clearly distinguished notes; do not imply guaranteed scholarship. Documents should reflect the required commitment letter and video and make nationality documents conditional. This is a change proposal only; catalog is not edited.
- No factual contradiction found between the Chinese and English versions for the key application dates, fee, duration, language or eligibility reviewed. The English page gives a passport-validity criterion (beyond 2027-03-31) tied to the 2026 notice; do not mistake that date for an admission-cycle date.
