## Corrections to research owners

1. **R09A:** change `language.Chinese/HSK` from “not applicable/no requirement” to a split statement: official handbook says HSK 4 (180+) for English-taught bachelor degree students, while its timing/role is not specified and the English admission page omits it. Keep admission applicability unknown pending UIBE confirmation. Add [U2] and do not assign 2026/2027 admission status without a cycle-specific rule.
2. **R09B:** change BGS HSK 4 from an undifferentiated degree-study condition to `conflict/applicability_unclear` for the English route. The official Beijing 2026 procedure applies HSK 4 to Chinese-taught degree programs and requires foreign-language proficiency for foreign-language programs; Category A English evidence is TOEFL 85/IELTS 6.5/equivalent. UIBE may impose more; its specific rule and whether this major is eligible still need confirmation. Reclassify the 2026 deadline as `source_unreachable` until the official notice itself can be opened.
3. **R10A/R10B:** keep the route-specific CNY 12,000 bundle and general brochure CNY 21,000 tuition plus CNY 750/month/person double room as a live unresolved conflict; do not represent either as a reconciled payable price. The scholarship guide's maximum waiver mechanism may be described, but its monetary maximum cannot be calculated from the conflicting prices.
4. **R10B:** preserve the Chinese/English HSK validity discrepancy and the different concurrent-scholarship wording as conflicts; no translation should add an application prohibition unless SDUFE confirms it.

Corrections are requests only. They have not been applied to the R09/R10 evidence or reports. All fields lacking direct cycle-specific support remain unknown and must not be marked verified in D02. No unresolved conflict above is accepted as a single confirmed value.
