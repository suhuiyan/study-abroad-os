# Product Foundation

## Product
Study Abroad OS

## First market
Thailand.

## First user
Thai students exploring or applying for overseas undergraduate study, especially those who want scholarships and want to manage the process themselves.

## Core problem
The study-abroad process is fragmented and complex. Students must combine information from search engines, university sites, scholarship sites, social groups, agencies, spreadsheets, AI tools, and seniors. They often do not know which information applies to their profile, what to do next, or which sources to trust.

### Problem layers
- Information fragmentation: universities, programs, scholarships, deadlines, visas, costs, housing
- Decision complexity: where can I apply, which option is best, can I afford it
- Execution complexity: what comes next, which document is missing, which deadline matters
- Trust: is information official, fresh, unbiased, safe
- Social uncertainty: no senior to ask, no peers going to the same place

## Desired outcome
The user should be able to answer:
- Where can I realistically apply?
- Which scholarships fit me?
- What will it cost?
- What do I need to do next?
- What am I missing?
- Who can help if I get stuck?
- Who else is going to the same university or city?

## Core emotional outcomes
Clarity, confidence, control, trust, belonging.

## Existing alternatives
Google, university websites, scholarship databases, ChatGPT, Reddit, Facebook/LINE/Discord groups, spreadsheets/Notion, education agencies, school counsellors, friends, parents, seniors, IDP, Yocket, Studyportals, Unibuddy, Goin'.

## Product thesis
If reliable study-abroad data is connected to a student's profile and converted into a personalized action plan, with AI, seniors, and community available when needed, students can manage more of the process themselves without depending on full-service agencies for every step.
