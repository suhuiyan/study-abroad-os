# Trust, Safety & Verification

## Trust metadata
Critical records should support:
- source_url
- source_type
- retrieved_at
- last_checked_at
- verified_at
- verified_by
- version
- confidence/status

## User/mentor verification
Possible badges:
- Identity Verified
- University Email/Student Verified
- Admitted Verified
- Current Student Verified
- Alumni Verified
- Scholarship Verified

Sensitive documents should not be exposed publicly.

## Community safeguards
- block/report
- moderation
- privacy controls
- do not expose precise location by default
- discourage moving sensitive conversations/payment off-platform

## Minor safeguards
Some users may be under 18. Design stricter defaults for contact, privacy, direct messaging, and in-person meetup features. Avoid exposing phone/address/location by default.

## Housing principles
- verified first, sponsored second
- clearly label sponsored listings
- support scam reports and risk flags
- show last verification date
- disclose rent, deposit, utilities and fees clearly
- never imply absolute safety guarantees
