# PRD v0.1

## Product definition
A self-service Study Abroad OS that helps Thai students discover universities and scholarships, understand eligibility, manage applications, get trusted help, and connect with relevant student communities throughout the study-abroad journey.

## Positioning
Not merely a scholarship database, AI chatbot, or digital agency. The product is a personalized study-abroad journey platform.

## Core pillars
### Discover
University, program, scholarship search.

### Decide
Eligibility, matching, comparison, cost and trust signals.

### Apply
Application journey, documents, tasks, deadlines.

### Get Help
AI assistance and on-demand verified student mentors.

### Connect
Utility-first communities linked to university, intake, program, nationality or journey stage.

### Live Abroad
Visa support, safer housing, roommate discovery and arrival support in later phases.

## Core product loop
Create Profile -> Discover -> Match -> Save -> Start Journey -> Complete Tasks -> Track Progress -> Get Offer -> Join Community -> Arrive -> Become Mentor/Alumni.

- Stories/reels/followers/likes
- Flight booking
- Banking
- Student loans
- Full insurance marketplace
- End-to-end application submission to every university
- Full-service agency operations

## Product promise
From confusion to a clear path that the student can follow step by step.
