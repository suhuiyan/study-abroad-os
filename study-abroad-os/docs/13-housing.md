# Safer Student Housing

## Goal
Help international students find a wider variety of off-campus housing with stronger trust signals and lower scam risk.

## Listing data
- property/provider
- rent
- deposit
- utilities
- fees
- commute/distance to university
- availability
- contract length
- photos/video
- last verified date

## Trust signals
- Property Verified
- Provider Verified
- Student Stayed Here
- Student Reviews
- Contract details/review where supported
- Scam reports / risk flags

## Community integration
Show relevant community context such as students from the same university who have lived there or are seeking roommates, subject to privacy safeguards.

## Business rule
Verified first, sponsored second. Sponsorship must be clearly labeled and must not override safety/trust ranking.
