# Jobs To Be Done

## Primary JTBD
When I want to study abroad but do not know where to start, I want to see options that fit my profile and a clear sequence of what I need to do, so I can apply confidently by myself.

## Stage-based JTBD
- Exploring: help me understand which countries and universities fit my goals and budget.
- Shortlisting: tell me where my profile meets or misses requirements.
- Scholarship: show me scholarships I am actually eligible to pursue.
- Application: tell me what documents, tasks, and deadlines apply to me.
- Decision: help me compare offers, cost, scholarships, and trade-offs.
- Pre-departure: help me handle visa, housing, travel, and preparation.
- Community: help me meet people going to the same university/intake and trusted seniors.
- Help: if I get stuck, connect me with AI or a verified senior who has relevant experience.
- Alumni: let me help the next cohort and optionally earn money as a mentor.

## Emotional JTBD
- I want to feel that I know what I am doing.
- I want confidence that I am not missing anything important.
- I want more control over the process.
- I want trusted help without being forced into a large package.
- I do not want to feel alone before moving abroad.
